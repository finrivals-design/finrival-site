Market discovery

Search the supported catalog, review movers, market sessions, activity and category views without turning the home screen into a terminal.

▤

Financial news

Browse market-focused stories, keep the source visible and use supported news context as a starting point for further research.

⇄

Save assets, revisit them quickly and use supported price-alert workflows without pretending the app is an execution venue.
