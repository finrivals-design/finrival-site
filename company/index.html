01

Connected

02

Focused

Important information is prioritized instead of filling every screen with unnecessary data.

03

Mobile-first

The workflow is designed around focused research on iOS and Android, not a compressed desktop terminal.

04

Clear boundaries

Research, AI assistance and simulation are visually and functionally separated from real-money execution.
