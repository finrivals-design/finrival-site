ABOUT FINRIVAL

Less noise.
More research clarity.

Finrival is being built around a simple product idea: market research should feel connected, focused and understandable without pretending uncertainty does not exist.

PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
“Make the next research step obvious.”

Discovery should lead naturally to comparison. Comparison should lead to explanation. Useful ideas should be easy to save and revisit.

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

A focused finance product, not a trading venue.

The product deliberately separates research, AI-assisted context and simulation from real-money execution.

◫

Research first

Finrival is organized around market discovery, comparison, news, saved assets and structured follow-up.

◆

AI with boundaries

AI can help structure research, but important facts still need independent verification.

★

Simulation stays fictional

Rival Credits have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn and are not real investment funds.

✓

Clear disclosures

Delayed or cached data, model limitations and product boundaries are made visible instead of hidden in marketing copy.
