How to use these guides

Every guide ends with a way to try the idea yourself in the Web Terminal or the Finrival app, using real end-of-day prices. If you want to practise a decision, Rival gives you 100,000 fictional Rival Credits, so mistakes cost nothing.

More guides are on the way: trading activity and volume, ETFs, dividends, risk and volatility, the P/E ratio, earnings reports, stocks compared with crypto, and practising without real money.
