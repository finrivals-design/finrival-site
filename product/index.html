WHAT IS FINRIVAL?

One workspace instead of disconnected research tools.

Financial research often means jumping between market screens, charting tools, news feeds, watchlists and separate AI products.

Finrival is designed to connect those steps. Start with the market, move into deeper comparison, continue with relevant context and keep important assets organized without leaving the same workspace.
