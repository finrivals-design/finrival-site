WHAT IS FINRIVAL?

One workspace instead of disconnected research tools.

Financial research often means jumping between market screens, charting tools, news feeds, watchlists and separate AI products.

Finrival is designed to connect those steps. Start with the market, move into deeper comparison, continue with relevant context and keep important assets organized without leaving the same workspace.

THE IDEA

Context before complexity.

Finrival is designed to make deeper research accessible without turning the experience into a professional trading terminal.

01

Connected

Research can move naturally between market, comparison, news, AI and monitoring.

02

Focused

Important information is prioritized instead of filling every screen with unnecessary data.

03

Mobile-first

Finrival is designed around a focused mobile research workflow for iOS and Android.

04

Clear boundaries

Research, AI assistance and simulation are visually and functionally separated from real-money execution.
