Connected workflow

Discover. Compare. Understand. Monitor.

This is the shortest honest description of the product: find what matters, inspect it in context, continue the question and keep the important assets close.

01 · Discover

Market and News

Start with market movement, liquidity, sessions or a story that needs follow-up.

02 · Compare

Compare or Rival

Move from a broad idea into side-by-side structure, simulation or relative context.

03 · Understand

Finrival AI

Keep the context attached when you ask for a short explanation or follow-up insight.

04 · Monitor

Watchlist

Save the assets that still matter and set reference-price alerts where useful.
