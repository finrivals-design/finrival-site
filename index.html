How Finrival works

From market noise to usable context.

Finrival keeps the workflow simple: discover what matters, compare alternatives, add context and monitor what you care about.

01 · DISCOVER
Start with the market.
Browse market context, financial news and the assets you want to investigate further.
02 · COMPARE
Put alternatives side by side.
Explore historical behavior, momentum, risk and supported scenario views inside one comparison flow.
03 · UNDERSTAND
Add AI-assisted context.
Ask Finrival AI about supported asset, comparison and news context while keeping clear informational boundaries.
04 · MONITOR
Keep the important assets close.
Save assets to your Watchlist and configure reference-price alerts around the context you follow.

Clear product boundaries

Research software, not a brokerage.

Finrival is built for informational, educational and simulation use. It does not execute real-money securities or crypto orders and does not hold customer funds.

✓
Market information shown inside the app may be delayed, cached, stale, incomplete or inaccurate.
✓
Finrival AI is informational and may generate incorrect, incomplete or outdated output.
✓
Rival Credits are fictional, non-transferable and have no real-world monetary value.
✓
Users remain responsible for independently verifying information before making financial decisions.
Financial information disclaimer

Finrival is not a broker, investment adviser, bank, exchange or fiduciary. Nothing on this website or in the application constitutes personalized financial, investment, tax or legal advice, or an offer to buy or sell a financial instrument.
