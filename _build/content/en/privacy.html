Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Finrival may collect, use, store, disclose and protect information when you use the Finrival mobile application, related web pages, account features, Finrival AI, market-data features, watchlists, alerts, PRO features and other services we make available.

It does not replace the privacy terms of Apple, Google or other independent third-party services that you choose to use.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use Finrival, we may process the following categories of information.

Account and authentication

  • Email address.
  • Firebase authentication identifier and account status.
  • Email-verification status.
  • Authentication and security information needed to sign in, maintain a session, protect an account and process account deletion.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

Watchlist, alerts and app preferences

  • Assets you add to your watchlist.
  • Price-alert settings, including the selected asset, alert direction and target reference price.
  • App preferences and locally stored settings.
  • Language and interface preferences.
  • PRO entitlement or subscription status after store billing is enabled.

Finrival AI

When you use Finrival AI, we may process:

  • The text you submit to Finrival AI.
  • The financial asset, market, comparison or news context associated with your request.
  • AI conversation or session information needed to provide the requested feature.
  • Technical metadata needed for authentication, quota enforcement, abuse prevention, reliability and security.
Do not submit highly sensitive personal information, passwords, payment-card details, private keys, seed phrases, confidential business secrets, medical information or other information that is not necessary for your Finrival AI request.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, we may process:

  • Device push-notification tokens.
  • Platform and technical information needed to deliver alerts.
  • Price-alert configuration associated with your account.

You can control notification permission in your device settings.

Technical, diagnostic and security information

  • Device or operating-system information.
  • App version.
  • IP address and approximate network information, where automatically processed by hosting, security, advertising or service providers.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Error, security and abuse-prevention information.
  • Server logs needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot Finrival.

We do not intentionally collect precise GPS location unless a future feature clearly requests it and you grant the required permission.

Advertising and advertising consent

If advertisements are enabled for Free users, advertising providers such as Google AdMob may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted.
  • IP address.
  • Device and app information.
  • Ad interaction and measurement information.
  • Consent choices.
  • Coarse location or other signals derived by the advertising provider, where permitted by law and platform rules.

The exact data processed by an advertising provider depends on your region, device settings, consent choices, platform permissions and the provider's own policies.

  • Entitlement status.

Finrival AI and your privacy

Finrival AI is an automated, AI-assisted information feature. When you submit a prompt:

  • Your prompt may be sent through Finrival's backend to an AI service provider.
  • Relevant market, comparison, asset or news context may be included.
  • Authentication and quota information may be processed to protect the service and enforce usage limits.
  • AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete or outdated.

Do not include anything in a prompt that you do not want processed in order to generate a response. Finrival does not represent that AI output is confidential professional advice.

Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal, security and technical requirements.

  • Account-linked data may be kept while your account remains active.
  • Watchlist and alert data may be kept while the relevant feature or account remains active.
  • Push tokens may be kept while needed to provide notifications, and may be removed when no longer valid or when the related account is deleted.
  • Security and server logs may be kept for a limited period where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and legal compliance.
  • Purchase or entitlement records may be kept as necessary to recognise purchased access, resolve billing issues, prevent fraud or comply with legal obligations.
  • Data that must legally be retained may be kept for the required period.

When an account-deletion request is completed, Finrival deletes or disassociates account-linked personal data that we are not legally required or otherwise permitted to retain. Backups, logs or distributed systems may need a limited period before deleted information is fully removed from all technical copies.

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your Finrival account from within the app: Control Center → Account / Security → Delete Account & Data. The app may ask you to sign in again or complete other reasonable security checks first, to prevent unauthorised account removal.

You can also request deletion without reinstalling the app at finrival.com/delete-account, or by emailing support@finrival.com with the subject "Finrival Account Deletion Request". Where possible, send the request from the email address linked to your account. We may ask for reasonable verification before deleting an account.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Subscriptions must be managed through the applicable store account.
