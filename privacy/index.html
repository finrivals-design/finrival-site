03

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use Finrival, we may process the following categories of information.

A. Account and authentication information

  • Email address.
  • Firebase authentication identifier and account status.
  • Email-verification status.
  • Authentication and security information necessary to sign in, maintain a session, protect an account and process account deletion.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

B. Watchlist, alert and app-preference information

  • Assets you add to your Watchlist.
  • Price-alert settings, including selected asset, alert direction and target reference price.
  • App preferences and locally stored settings.
  • Language and interface preferences.
  • PRO entitlement or subscription status after store billing is enabled.

C. Finrival AI information

When you use Finrival AI, we may process:

  • The text you submit to Finrival AI.
  • The financial asset, market, comparison or news context associated with your request.
  • AI conversation or session information necessary to provide the requested feature.
  • Technical metadata needed for authentication, quota enforcement, abuse prevention, reliability and security.
Do not submit highly sensitive personal information, passwords, payment-card details, private keys, seed phrases, confidential business secrets, medical information or other information that is not necessary for your Finrival AI request.

D. Push-notification information

If you enable notifications, we may process:

  • Device push-notification tokens.
  • Platform and technical information needed to deliver alerts.
  • Price-alert configuration associated with your account.

You can control notification permission through your device settings.

E. Technical, diagnostic and security information

  • Device or operating-system information.
  • App version.
  • IP address and approximate network information where automatically processed by hosting, security, advertising or service providers.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Error, security and abuse-prevention information.
  • Server logs necessary to operate, protect and troubleshoot Finrival.

We do not intentionally collect precise GPS location unless a future feature clearly requests it and you grant the required permission.

F. Advertising and advertising-consent information

If advertisements are enabled for Free users, advertising providers such as Google AdMob may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers where permitted.
  • IP address.
  • Device and app information.
  • Ad interaction and measurement information.
  • Consent choices.
  • Coarse location or other signals derived by the advertising provider where permitted by law and platform rules.

The exact data processed by an advertising provider depends on your region, device settings, consent choices, platform permissions and the provider's own policies.

G. Purchase and subscription information

Finrival does not directly collect or store your full payment-card details.

If you purchase Finrival PRO, Apple App Store or Google Play processes the transaction. Finrival may receive limited purchase or entitlement information needed to determine whether your PRO access is active, such as:

  • Product or subscription identifier.
  • Transaction or purchase-token information.
  • Entitlement status.
  • Subscription status and expiration information where provided by the store.

08

Finrival AI privacy

Finrival AI is an automated AI-assisted information feature.

When you submit a prompt:

  • Your prompt may be sent through Finrival's backend to an AI service provider.
  • Relevant market, comparison, asset or news context may be included.
  • Authentication and quota information may be processed to protect the service and enforce usage limits.

09

Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to legal, security and technical requirements.

  • Account-linked data may be retained while your account remains active.
  • Watchlist and alert data may be retained while the relevant feature or account remains active.
  • Push tokens may be retained while needed to provide notifications and may be removed when no longer valid or when the related account is deleted.
  • Security and server logs may be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and legal compliance.
  • Purchase or entitlement records may be retained as necessary to recognize purchased access, resolve billing issues, prevent fraud or comply with legal obligations.
  • Data legally required to be retained may be kept for the required period.

When an account-deletion request is completed, Finrival deletes or disassociates account-linked personal data that we are not legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

Backups, logs or distributed systems may require a limited period before deleted information is fully removed from all technical copies.

10

Account and data deletion

You may permanently delete your Finrival account from within the application.

In-app deletion path: Control Center → Account / Security → Delete Account.

The app may require re-authentication or other reasonable security verification before deletion to prevent unauthorized account removal.

You may also request account deletion without reinstalling the app by using: finrival.com/delete-account .

You may alternatively send a deletion request to finrivals@gmail.com with the subject "Finrival Account Deletion Request".

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Finrival account where possible. We may ask for reasonable verification before deleting an account.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Subscriptions must be managed through the applicable store account.
