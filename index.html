How Finrival works

From market noise to usable context.

Finrival keeps the workflow simple: discover what matters, compare alternatives, add context and monitor what you care about.

01 · DISCOVER

Start with the market.

Browse market context, supported assets and financial news through a research-first interface.

02 · COMPARE

Put alternatives side by side.

Explore historical behavior, momentum, risk and supported scenario-based views.

03 · UNDERSTAND

Add AI-assisted context.

Open the global Finrival AI assistant from anywhere in the app and continue supported research in context.

04 · MONITOR

Keep important assets close.

Save assets to your Watchlist and configure reference-price alerts around your research flow.

Product tour

Five core tabs. One source-matched AI layer.

The phone on the left is a second instance of the same code-driven mobile replica used in the hero. Select any explanation to switch that exact replica to Home, News, Compare, Rival, Watchlist or the global Finrival AI modal. The replica is intentionally larger here so the interface remains readable without changing its native proportions.

01 · Home

Market discovery comes first.

Top Gainer / Top Dip, Daily Liquidity, anomaly context, market freshness, market sessions and the All Assets list.

02 · News

Browse stories with source and time context.

Timeline-style financial news cards keep the feed compact and provide a path into supported AI follow-up.

03 · Compare

The raised center action.

Compare is intentionally emphasized in the mobile navigation and opens historical performance, chart and RSI-style context.

04 · Rival

Simulation stays visually distinct.

Gold treatment separates fictional Rival Credits and simulated activity from normal market research.

05 · Watchlist

Save assets and create reference alerts.

Watchlist keeps saved assets, one-time reference-price alerts and AI shortcuts in a dedicated monitoring tab.

GLOBAL · Finrival AI

Available above the navigation.

The AI assistant is not another tab. It floats above the app, then opens into a blurred modal experience with its own header, chat and composer.

Clear product boundaries

Research software, not a brokerage.

Finrival is built for informational, educational and simulation use. It does not execute real-money securities or crypto orders and does not hold customer funds.

✓Market information shown inside the app may be delayed, cached, stale, incomplete or inaccurate.
✓Finrival AI is informational and may generate incorrect, incomplete or outdated output.
✓Rival Credits are fictional, non-transferable and have no real-world monetary value.
✓Users remain responsible for independently verifying information before making financial decisions.
